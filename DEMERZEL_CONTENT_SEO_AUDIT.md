# DEMERZEL content and SEO audit

## Source of truth
- Primary catalogue content is grounded in the Vanrakshak optics dataset in src/data/products.ts.
- Application and solution framing is grounded in src/data/solutions.ts.
- The site should present DEMERZEL as a catalogue-oriented equipment reference, not as a broad ecommerce or AI-style marketing site.

## Major pages reviewed
- Homepage
- Products catalogue
- Product detail pages
- Brands and brand detail pages
- Solutions and solution detail pages
- About
- Contact
- Industries
- Field
- Resources
- Projects

## Primary themes
- Homepage: DEMERZEL catalogue overview, Vanrakshak optics, product discovery, application discovery.
- Products: product catalogue and filterable equipment reference.
- Product detail: specific product family, specifications and relevant applications.
- Brand pages: brand-specific product families from the catalogued source data.
- Application pages: field use cases such as wildlife, forestry, security, survey, research and outdoor use.

## Content direction applied
- Reduced generic corporate language.
- Removed future-facing wording that was not backed by the catalogue.
- Tightened titles, descriptions and headings around real product and application terms.
- Replaced placeholder or invented contact language with factual enquiry-based copy.
